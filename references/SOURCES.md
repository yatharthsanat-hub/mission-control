## Instructions for Development

- Review the local PDFs and accessible websites before developing the mission simulation.
- Use NASA engineering concepts to inform spacecraft design and mission trade-offs.
- Clearly identify educational assumptions rather than presenting invented values as NASA specifications.
- Record which sources were actually accessed.
- Do not copy protected graphics or use NASA logos without authorization.
- Document how the NASA resources were used in the finished game.
