# MISSION CONTROL 2.0 product specification

Architecture proposal · November 14, 2026. The player directs a fictional space agency and completes a cycle of funding, requirements, assembly, planning, flight, and science review. A first mission should teach one end-to-end trade-off within a short session. The same mission can then be revised against the same environment and requirements.

## Workspaces

| Workspace | Player decisions and feedback | Handoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agency Headquarters | Name an agency, open or create a local save, review cash and committed funding, fund one research project, select a contract and manage a portfolio. A research project has prerequisites, a USD cost and a duration in program days. Advancing the program calendar completes earned milestones and unlocks specific catalog entries. | Funded contract and mission draft |
| Vehicle Assembly Building | Select parts and compatible nodes in a 3D workbench. Inspect a part, snap it to a mount, rotate about the mount using allowed increments, remove a subtree, undo or redo. Inspect dry/wet mass, center of mass, cost, mode power and usable delta-v after each edit. Show when an estimate needs an input. | Assembly revision with validation findings |
| Mission Planning | Trace the user’s science question to measurable deliverables. Select a launch offer, deployment state, allowed orbit and maneuver plan. Compare geometry-derived passes, target windows, worst energy margin, propellant reserve, link margin and funded cost. Resolve blockers before committing. | Immutable approved plan, initial conditions and USD commitments |
| Mission Control | Play, pause, step or scrub. Schedule supported actions, such as deferring an observation, switching to a lower-rate mode or entering safe mode. Commands require a planning opportunity or modeled uplink. Inspect actual and forecast events alongside calculated positions. | Run log, telemetry and delivered products |
| Science and Program Review | Report each requirement as met, missed or unevaluable. Trace missed products to geometry, pointing, power, thermal inhibits, incomplete downlink or another recorded cause. Show financial commitments versus actuals, close the contract once, and offer a new revision for comparison. | Archived review, ledger settlement and progression |

Keep a single mission context across workspaces. Separate the program calendar, mission time, and display playback. A paused animation has no effect on balances or physics. Upgrades unlock parts or capabilities with documented properties. They never change universal constants. Repeat simulations are free planning exercises. A funded execution consumes its commitments and can settle once. Offer a low-cost training contract after a financial dead end. Use explicit contract grants and acceptance milestones with funded payouts, with no reward for replaying an archived attempt. Launch remains blocked for rejected or out-of-scope physics and an unfunded commitment.

## Portfolio

| Family and priority | Deliverable and hard constraints | Main trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Earth observation, first release | One curated LEO survey. Return complete geolocated image tiles for an area of interest with required spectral bands, illumination, maximum ground sample distance and a delivery deadline. Measure unique valid tiles against the contract target. A rideshare supplies a bounded initial state. Optional short burns change the local orbit within supported cases. | Image detail and target opportunities versus electrical energy, onboard storage and finite station contact. Lower-altitude detail costs coverage width for a fixed sensor. |
| Lunar reconnaissance, next release | Contracted delivery to a reviewed Moon-centered bound arrival ellipse. Execute a player-planned short insertion/circularization burn into a mapping orbit and retain required propellant. Return optical tiles of sunlit crater terrain with incidence and sample-distance limits. Require Earth station visibility, lunar occultation checks and positive link margin. Include one-way light time for communications. | Propulsion mass and mapping altitude versus downlink range, illumination and time on target. A visible terrain image earns no claim about buried ice or permanently shadowed terrain. |
| Atmospheric science, future | Limb/tangent geometry, atmospheric profiles and retrieval validation. Supports a different measurement and quality contract. | Geometry and calibration versus sampling |
| Asteroid reconnaissance, future | Small-body ephemerides, irregular gravity/shape, navigation and phase-angle limits. | Proximity and coverage versus navigation risk |
| Mars exploration, future | Interplanetary arrival and communications delay, Mars gravity and mission-specific instruments. Entry, descent and landing would need a separate model. | Transport, communications and autonomy |

Lunar arrival is a service boundary. Its supplied state has provenance, epoch, frame, mass and entrance conditions. Von Braun-style ascent, unrestricted transfers, unbound capture, third-body targeting, operational orbit determination, live traffic/debris and high-fidelity climate or terrain inference are outside the baseline. The heritage CubeSat tutorial remains playable under its original rules. [NASA Landsat 9](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/landsat-9/) · [NASA Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/lro/)

## USD economy

The first catalog contains fictional planning scenarios in fixed scenario-year USD. Build each estimate bottom-up with versioned rates and quantities. Example Earth study inputs are $140,000 of hardware, 800 integration and test hours at $100/hr, $60,000 for the scenario launch service, 100 ground hours at $200/hr, and 400 operations hours at $100/hr. This gives a $340,000 estimate with a 20% planning reserve of $68,000 and a $408,000 commitment requirement. Every input and resulting total in this example is illustrative. The structure follows work-package costing. Fixed-year dollars, no inflation or tax modeling, no implication that quotes can be obtained at these rates. [NASA Cost Estimating Handbook, Appendix B](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/ceh_appb.pdf)

Tag published context and scenario estimates separately. Published costs require exact provider/document, date and dollar year, contract scope and exclusions, quantity basis and link. Keep advertised minima, signed quotes and total lifecycle costs distinct. Display a dated context item beside the estimate only when scope is understood. No price from the rideshare page was used because the page yielded no readable pricing. [SpaceX rideshare](https://www.spacex.com/rideshare/)

Agency accounting uses integer USD cents. Cash = grants and settlements received − actual expenses paid. Available = cash − outstanding commitments. Debiting an approved expense reduces the corresponding commitment at the same time. Unused reserve is released at closure. Validate safe integers, nonnegative inputs and sufficient funding. Forecast, committed and paid are distinct UI states. Legacy credits stay in the V1 tutorial and have no exchange rate.
