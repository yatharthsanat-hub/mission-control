### Target geometry

Create a unit surface normal from signed spherical latitude/longitude. Accept latitude within ±90° and wrapped longitude within ±360°. The poles use longitude-independent normals. At Earth radius R the site is R × normal. Slant range is the length of the spacecraft-minus-site vector. Elevation is arcsin of line-of-sight dot the site normal. Off-nadir is the angle between the inward spacecraft radial direction and the target line of sight. Require positive elevation and off-nadir within the hardware slew bound.

The inscribed cone has full angle 2 atan(N × tan(IFOV) / 2), where N is the shorter detector dimension in pixels. Require the whole cone to clear the spherical limb. With slant range d and elevation ε, use the local tangent-plane footprint dimensions 2d tan(FOV/2) across the look direction and that value divided by sin(ε) along it. The upper sampling estimate is d tan(IFOV) / sin(ε). The window checks a 10 km minimum across-track footprint and an along-track extent no greater than the diameter of the scenario region. Solar elevation at the target is computed using the fixed Sun vector rotated to Earth-fixed axes. A rejected interval is assigned its first failing geometry or lighting rule. Footprints and GSD are reported at the best qualifying sample in the window. The footprint is approximate, and a scene is a time-distinct view centered on the target.

### Time, electrical and communications accounting

Evaluate each five-second bin at its midpoint for 24 hours, then aggregate contiguous bins. Timing resolution is five seconds, and marginal or shorter windows can be missed. Geometry drives both ground contacts and solar illumination. Generation and demand are held constant within each bin. Add the net electrical energy (generation minus demand, in W, times duration in seconds) to storage, applying charge or discharge efficiency on the corresponding side and bounding charge to [0, capacity]. Keep unserved and curtailed energy separately. Neither the array incidence nor the battery voltage, aging or temperature is modeled.

Forecast one powered and storable capture per qualifying contiguous window, up to the quota, and apply the spectral gate before capturing. Housekeeping has priority. With ground contact and stored data, prioritize powered downlink for that bin, otherwise consider imaging. Capture draws the imager and any mounted fine-pointing load. Downlink uses the mounted radio load for a full bin. A scene is initially transmitted in FIFO order, can span contact bins, and counts only once all its bits have been sent. Geometry capacity is a ceiling based on total sampled contact duration × capped rate, so it can include time when the memory is empty or the battery precludes transmission. Forecast outputs are feasibility estimates. This phase produces no executed mission telemetry.

Approval fails on a demonstrated shortage of target windows, housekeeping power, contacts, or complete forecast deliveries, as well as inherited hardware, launch, spectral, and funding blockers. A minimum charge of 15% or less warns. The RF link budget is **NOT ASSESSED** because transmitter power, gains, losses and receiver performance are unavailable. The fictional service permits conditional training approval only after the player explicitly acknowledges this gap. Changing the plan invalidates the acknowledgement. Real RF feasibility remains unresolved.
