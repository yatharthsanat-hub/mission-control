## Original inventory and integration matrix

**A · DIRECT NASA DATA** means a published NASA/JPL quantity or dataset enters a calculation, requirement or reference fixture. **B · NASA-DERIVED SCIENTIFIC MODEL** means a sourced modeling relationship or a NASA tool that checks the scoped physics. **C · NASA ENGINEERING GUIDANCE** means a process, interface concept or evidence rule. **D · NASA VISUAL OR EDUCATIONAL INSPIRATION** means a presentation or learning pattern. CSA is marked separately as partner inspiration. These contribution categories are independent of the [scientific implementation statuses](SCIENTIFIC_VALIDATION.md).

Store each numerical input with original value/unit, locator and source revision, then normalize once. Where applicable also record center, axes/frame, epoch, time scale, interpolation, coverage interval and artifact hash. Derivations carry model revision and input IDs. A published source datum keeps its original definition. A benchmark comparison uses an independent tool/artifact with separate output provenance. Data used only as a benchmark or educational citation should be identified as such in the UI.

### Unsupported claims and open evidence

- Band overlap or coverage alone cannot demonstrate crop health or flooding. First-release science evaluates the delivered imaging product against the contract. Reflectance retrieval, cloud masks, georegistration accuracy, radiometric equivalence and physical crop or water inference need additional models and evidence.
- Lunar pixel scale is a sampling metric. Image quality, topographic accuracy, water/ice detection, or usable imaging in permanent shadow need separate evidence. An arrival ellipse is a service input. No completed lunar transfer, actual Earth station availability or true LRO operations replay is implied.
- No V2 GMAT reference run, NAIF kernel set, source-versioned telemetry, current ICD, or flight-quality component/terminal data was verified here. Freeze a reproducible comparison case and confirm units/frames before claiming a pass. The existing tested game loop is illustrative.

## Competition evidence and attribution

- The [local challenge brief](../references/CHALLENGE.md) calls for a playable science-to-redesign loop and credible trade-offs with sourced facts and labeled game values. The [local submission checklist](../references/SUBMISSION_REQUIREMENTS.md) requires the project name, 50–200 word summary, public project link, project details, named exact resource URLs with material-use explanations, and public demo link. Its Global Award demo limit is a 30-second video or seven-slide deck. The official 2026 specific challenge and direct participant terms URL returned 502. An indexed copy of the [2026 Participant Terms](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/legal/) stated source citation and license documentation requirements. The current form, exact challenge wording, and portal instructions remain unverified. Neither local file establishes a minimum number of NASA resources or mandates GMAT, real imagery or VR.
- Submit a short evidence list with source title, exact working URL, locator/version, role, and the actual decision/result it affects. For datasets include provider and product, DOI or identifier where assigned, version, relevant time/location, license/terms, and transforms. For code/assets track license, creator, credit and permissions. Follow the applicable [NASA Images and Media Guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/) and check each image's credit and usage conditions, especially third-party material. Use original visuals and no NASA branding. A source being readable or open does not settle permissions for unrelated assets on its page.
- Disclose AI assistance used in research and architecture, and update the disclosure to cover actual code and data development. Under the local checklist, label AI-generated image/video with the required visible SI-generated watermark, acknowledge AI-generated audio in text or metadata, and exclude NASA branding, logos, flags and mission identifiers from AI-generated media. Check current portal wording before submission.
- Judges should be able to open the game and demo without login, repeat the three illustrated decisions, inspect the affected value and its provenance, and see whether the outcome changes. Report a benchmark pass only if the evidence is published or available for review. Use the submitted build's actual scope and verify final links and media length.
